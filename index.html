Quack Mayhem — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Developer: Buffster (independent developer, Québec, Canada)

Privacy contact: robert195505@pm.me

About the game

Quack Mayhem is a seated virtual-reality (VR) and mixed-reality (MR) carnival shooting game for Meta Quest, intended for players aged 10 and older. It includes offline single-player gameplay and optional Meta Horizon online leaderboard features. Online leaderboards are currently disabled while Meta app authorization, age-group access and private-channel testing are completed.

Information handled by the game

  • Game progress and settings: The game stores personal-best scores, round statistics and comfort/control settings locally on the headset. Older scoring records may be retained separately. Local performance diagnostic reports may include frame timings, graphics statistics and the headset model.
  • Quest interaction and mixed reality: To run the game, the headset processes controller actions, head position and other XR tracking information. MR uses Meta's passthrough functionality so you can see your surroundings. Quack Mayhem does not intentionally record, store or transmit passthrough camera images, microphone audio or room video to the developer.
  • Optional online features: If online leaderboards are enabled, the game uses Meta's Platform SDK to process a Meta account identifier, permitted public display name, completed 60-second-round score and leaderboard/rank information. Friends ranking information is requested only when the user's age category and Meta permissions allow it. When online services are enabled, the game requests Meta's age category each online session and checks it again before authorized ranking operations. It does not request a date of birth or exact age. The age category is kept only in memory for that session and is not written to the game's saved files or developer logs.
  • Contact messages: If you contact the developer by email, the information you provide in that message is used to answer your question or request.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate the game, retain your preferred settings and scores, respond to support/privacy inquiries, check eligibility for optional online features, submit eligible timed-round scores and show authorized rankings. Practice scores and historical records are not submitted as new leaderboard results.

Children and age-appropriate protections

Quack Mayhem is intended for ages 10 and older, including players aged 10–12. It is not intended for children under 10. For players in Meta's child category (10–12), Quack Mayhem currently keeps all public and Friends leaderboard reads and score submissions unavailable, as well as profile and identity reads for those features. Teen and adult online access requires a current age category, account entitlement and Meta permissions. If age or authorization cannot be established, online features stay unavailable while offline gameplay can continue. Session changes clear cached account and ranking information before access can be re-established. These safeguards require authorized account testing before public online availability.

Sharing and service providers

Meta provides the Quest platform and the optional leaderboard service. When an authorized leaderboard is used, related account and score information is handled through Meta's services under Meta's applicable policies. The developer does not operate an independent player-account or leaderboard server and does not sell player information. The game does not use third-party advertising or behavioral advertising. Store purchases, if offered, are handled through Meta rather than by the game developer.

Storage and retention

Local preferences, performance reports and score records remain on the device until they are cleared through the device's app-data controls or by uninstalling the game, subject to the headset's own backup behavior. The developer does not maintain a separate server copy of those local records. Meta's retention and deletion of any online leaderboard records are subject to Meta's own platform processes and policies. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to requests and for necessary records, then deleted when no longer needed.

When online features are enabled, account identifiers, permitted display names and rankings, age category, and an eligible current-round score submission are held only temporarily in application memory. The game does not maintain a persistent age cache or historical score-upload queue. Starting a new online session clears the previous account and ranking data. Meta's own online retention is governed by its policies.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can play without optional online leaderboards if they are unavailable. You can remove locally stored game data using the headset's app-data or uninstall controls; this may also remove personal-best scores and preferences. To ask questions, request access to information held by the developer, or request deletion, email robert195505@pm.me with “Quack Mayhem privacy request” in the subject. Please do not send a password, app secret, date of birth or other sensitive account credentials. If your request concerns information held by Meta, we will explain the relevant Meta account or privacy controls and assist where feasible.

Security and changes

The game is designed to minimize information processed outside the device and to use Meta's authorized platform services for online functions. This policy may be updated when online features or data practices change. The current version will remain accessible at the privacy-policy link provided in the Meta Horizon Store listing.
