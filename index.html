Quack Mayhem — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Developer and data controller: Robert Vandal, independent developer operating as BuffstersQuest, Québec, Canada

About Quack Mayhem

Quack Mayhem is a single-player carnival shooting game for Meta Quest, playable seated in virtual reality (VR) and mixed reality (MR). It is intended for people aged 10 and older. Core gameplay, local scores and settings work without an internet connection. The game also offers separate live Meta Horizon leaderboards for completed 60-second rounds played with one hand (1H) or two hands (2H), where the player's account, age category and Meta permissions allow access. Online leaderboards are not required to play offline.

Information handled by the game

  • Local gameplay and settings: The game saves personal-best scores, round statistics and comfort/control preferences on the headset. Older score records may be retained separately. Local performance diagnostics may contain frame timings, graphics statistics and headset model information. The developer does not receive these local files automatically.
  • Meta account and leaderboard information: For authorized online features, the Meta Platform SDK checks the current app session and entitlement and processes an app-scoped Meta account identifier, permitted public display name, the eligible 60-second-round score, leaderboard rank and ranking entries. The game has distinct 1H and 2H boards, with Global, Friends (when authorized by Meta) and My Ranking views. A submitted score and permitted public display name may appear to other eligible leaderboard viewers.
  • Age category: To restrict online features appropriately, the game requests a broad age category from Meta and validates eligibility for authorized ranking operations. It does not ask for a date of birth or exact age. The category and current online account/ranking information are used in session memory, not stored as a persistent age profile in the game's save files.
  • Headset, controllers and MR: The Quest system processes head/controller tracking and, for MR, passthrough imagery to provide the experience. Quack Mayhem does not intentionally record, save or transmit passthrough camera images, room video or microphone recordings to the developer.
  • Contact requests: If you email BuffstersQuest, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide so that we can respond.

How the information is used

Local records run the game and preserve settings and personal bests. Meta account context and broad age category determine whether a user can access online ranking services. Eligible, newly completed 60-second scores can be sent to the corresponding 1H or 2H Meta leaderboard; Meta retains the user's highest qualifying score on each board. Practice, interrupted rounds and historical local records are not silently submitted. Permitted account display names, scores and ranks are shown in authorized ranking views. Support messages are used to respond to inquiries and privacy requests.

Quack Mayhem does not use third-party advertising, behavioral advertising or an independent developer-operated player-account/leaderboard server. It does not sell player information.

Children and age-appropriate protections

The game is intended for ages 10 and older, including eligible Meta Quest users aged 10–12; it is not intended for children under 10. For Meta's child age category (10–12), the game does not allow leaderboard reads, friend rankings or score submissions and does not perform profile/identity reads for those leaderboard features. Teen and adult online access requires a current eligible age category, valid entitlement and the necessary Meta permissions. If age or authorization cannot be established, online features remain unavailable while offline gameplay can continue. Meta's own account and regional access rules also apply.

Sharing and service providers

Meta provides the Quest platform, app entitlement/age services and online leaderboards. Authorized account identifiers, permitted profile names and leaderboard results are processed through Meta's services, subject to Meta's privacy policy. BuffstersQuest does not maintain a separate online copy of players' scores or Meta profiles. Support emails are handled through our email providers for reply and reasonable recordkeeping. We do not share player information with advertisers.

Storage and retention

Local game saves, preferences, diagnostics and score records remain on the headset until removed using device app-data controls or uninstalling the game, subject to any Meta device backup/restore behavior. The developer cannot remotely erase headset files. Quack Mayhem does not keep a persistent age-category cache, a separate player account database or a historical online score-upload queue. Current online account/ranking details and any pending eligible round submission are held temporarily in application memory, and relevant session changes clear them.

Online leaderboard entries and associated account data are stored and retained by Meta according to its platform processes. Removing the game's local files does not by itself delete Meta-hosted entries. Support correspondence and related request records are retained only as reasonably necessary for support, privacy request handling and applicable recordkeeping obligations; copies held by email providers or in backups may have separate retention arrangements.

Your choices, access and deletion requests

You can play offline without using the online leaderboard views. To remove local scores and settings, use the headset's app-data management options or uninstall Quack Mayhem; this may erase saved progress. This does not automatically remove leaderboard entries already stored by Meta.

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by BuffstersQuest, or to ask us to help with a Quack Mayhem leaderboard entry, email buffstersquest-support.skating711@passfwd.com with “Quack Mayhem privacy request” in the subject. You may indicate whether your request concerns a local record, a 1H or 2H leaderboard entry, or support correspondence. Provide only the minimum information needed to locate the issue. Never send passwords, Meta login credentials, access tokens, App Secrets, dates of birth or other sensitive credentials.

Robert will review requests without a fee and without undue delay. For records under BuffstersQuest's control, we will handle valid correction or deletion requests subject to applicable legal obligations. For a Meta-hosted leaderboard entry, we must first establish that the requester is authorized to act for that account and identify the correct entry; a public display name or emailed claim alone does not prove ownership. Meta documents a method for deleting an individual leaderboard entry, but the supported identity-verification workflow for user requests is still being clarified with Meta. We will explain what can be verified, what action can safely be taken, and any outstanding Meta-controlled steps. We will not delete another person's score based on an unverified request or promise a deletion before confirmation. You may also use Meta's own privacy/account controls for information Meta holds independently.

Security, questions and policy updates

The game relies on Meta's authorized platform interfaces for online features and does not ask players to enter Meta login credentials into the game. To ask a privacy question or report a security concern, contact buffstersquest-support.skating711@passfwd.com. We will revise this page when features or verified data-handling procedures change. Earlier versions remain available in the public GitHub repository history.
